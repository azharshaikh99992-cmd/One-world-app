🎲 Ludo

Room code: paste it in Chat to invite friends to a table.

How to play
• Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out.
• A 6, a capture or reaching home gives an extra roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
• Landing on an opponent sends it back (not on ★ safe cells).
• Get all 4 pawns to the center with an exact count to win.
• vs AI: you are Red. Pass & Play: Red → Green → Yellow → Blue.
